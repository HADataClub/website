\section{Summary}\label{summary}

Provide a brief overview of the analysis objectives, key findings, and
main conclusions. This section should be concise and accessible to
non-technical stakeholders. Max 1 paragraph, no bullet points

\section{Introduction}\label{introduction}

Briefly describe the context and motivation for the analysis. What
problem are you trying to solve or what question are you trying to
answer? List the main objectives of the analysis. Max 1 paragraph, no
bullet points.

\section{Data}\label{data}

Describe where the data comes from, how it was collected, and any
relevant details about data provenance. Max 1 paragraph (usually a few
sentences unless the experimental design is described in detail), no
bullet points.

\section{Statistical Analysis}\label{statistical-analysis}

Describe the statistical methods and techniques used in this analysis.
Max 1 paragraph (usually a few sentences), no bullet points.

\section{Results}\label{results}

Present the results of the analysis in a clear and concise manner.
Usually there should be a brief summary of each statistical test with an
appropriate accompanying graph or table. For a multivariate model with
multiple predictors, present the results in a table with the predictor
names and the corresponding p-values, accompanied by a graph of the
relationship between each predictor and the response variable.

\section{References}\label{references}

List any references or citations used in the analysis.
